summary: "Old Town has served guests beneath the medieval fortress of Visoko since 2005, but it was known as a stop for tourists, not a place locals chose. We built a brand rooted in the place itself and its medieval Bosnian heritage, and carried it through every touchpoint, from the menu on the table to the reel on a phone."
challenge: "Old Town is a family restaurant with bungalows, set beneath Stari grad Visoki, the royal fortress of medieval Bosnia, looking out onto Visočica hill. After twenty years and a newly renovated interior, the owners wanted locals from Visoko, Sarajevo and Zenica coming for dinner, and families choosing it for birthdays, weddings and company evenings. They had a logo they wanted to keep, a limited budget, and no visual identity, website or social presence to build on."
process: "We started from one sentence: a family home beneath the old town, since 2005. Everything else followed from it, including the slogan \"Pod Starim gradom, od 2005.\" The brand draws on medieval Bosnia. The fortress above the restaurant was the seat of Bosnian kings, so we looked to the visual language of that time: the stećci, the carved medieval tombstones found across the country. From their rosettes, borders, spirals and vine motifs we drew a library of simplified ornaments, with the vine as the primary line. They are used on an 80/20 rule, contemporary first and heritage second, so the brand feels rooted without turning into folklore. The logo's round form comes from the fortress itself. At the top of Stari grad sits a round stone well, and its shape became the circle of the badge. We refined the logo's type and colour, then filmed the well from above with a drone and animated the logo rising out of it, so the mark and its origin appear in the same shot. The palette comes from the surroundings: walnut, linen, Visočica green and ember. Roboto Serif carries the headlines, Nunito Sans the text, and the tone of voice is warm and colloquial, like the host talking."
solution: "One shoot in October produced the brand video, drone footage of the fortress, food, interiors and a series of reels, all cut for both feed and full screen and scored with instrumental Bosnian folk music. A 24-page bilingual menu was designed to fit the restaurant's existing leather covers, with a group menu, a page for celebrations and bungalows, and an editorial spread on the history of Stari grad. The website was written before it was designed. The brand video leads the homepage, celebrations have their own page with an enquiry form, and the bungalows link straight to Booking.com. Around it we set up everything the brand needs to be found: a rebuilt Google Business Profile, a proper Facebook page linked to Instagram, domain email, and consent-based analytics."
results: "Within the first 14 days on Instagram, with no paid advertising, the new content reached 61,000 people and gathered 114,000 views, 75% of them from people who did not follow Old Town yet. A single reel reached over 65,000 views, and the profile grew by 230 followers in 15 days. Old Town now has one visual system that runs across print, web and social, built from its own place and history."
