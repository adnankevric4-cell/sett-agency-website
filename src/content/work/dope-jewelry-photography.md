summary: "The products arrived from Oslo. We set up studio production in Sarajevo and handled the shoot end to end, covering clean product photography for the Shopify store and styled compositions for social media, all delivered web-ready."
challenge: "The products arrived from Oslo. The brief was simple: shoot at the highest possible level, consistent with where the brand was positioned. Not luxury, but not far from it either. Everything had to be ready for web and social media from the moment it landed."
process: "We set up studio production in Sarajevo and handled the shoot end to end, from art direction through to final delivery. The output covered two distinct formats: clean product photography optimised for the Shopify store, and a separate set of styled multi-product compositions built for social media. Two different contexts, two different visual approaches, both within the same brand language."
solution: "Shooting in Sarajevo rather than Oslo was a logistical decision, but it put the responsibility for the visual output entirely on our side. There was no local team who already knew the brand to fall back on. We had the products, we had the brief, and we had to make the result speak for itself. The final images were delivered web-ready, in the correct formats, without a round of reshoots."
results: "The clean studio work sits at one end of the brand's visual range, where the launch campaign sits at the other. Where the campaign is moody and editorial, this set is precise and product-first: accurate colour, clear detail, and consistent framing that lets each piece be understood on its own. Between the two, the brand had both the atmosphere to sell a feeling and the clarity to sell a product."
