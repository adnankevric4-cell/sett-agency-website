summary: "A packaging and label system for a US consumer health brand spanning three product categories: care & beauty, health supplements, and pet care. Sixteen products held together by a colour architecture that communicates category at a glance."
challenge: "Advelop is a consumer health brand built for the US market, spanning three distinct product categories: care & beauty, health supplements, and pet care. The client came to us with a logo, a base set of colours, and a clear structure of three sub-brands. What they needed was a packaging and label system that could hold all three together as one company while making it obvious, at a glance, which category a product belonged to."
process: "We started by refining what was already there. The existing logo and colours were adjusted and tightened to work consistently across packaging, and then we assigned each product category its own colour: a purple-toned identity for care & beauty, teal for health supplements, and orange for pet care. That single decision did most of the heavy lifting. A customer scanning a shelf or a product page doesn't need to read anything to know whether they are looking at a sleep supplement, a face cream, or dog paw balm. The colour tells them first."
solution: "For health products, three things matter above all: clean, legible, and trustworthy. We built the label layout around that hierarchy. Product name large and clear, function stated plainly underneath, supporting details and dosage information ordered so nothing competes for attention. No clutter, no decorative noise. In a category where customers are making decisions about what goes into their body or onto their skin, visual restraint reads as credibility."
results: "Across the full project we designed sixteen products, each one fitting cleanly into its category's visual language while staying unmistakably part of the wider Advelop brand. The result is a system that scales: as Advelop adds products, the framework already tells them how each new label should look. The work isn't a set of sixteen designs. It is a system that produced sixteen designs and can produce the next sixteen the same way."
