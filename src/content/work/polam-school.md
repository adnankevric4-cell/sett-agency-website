summary: Polam School and Nursery had a warm, distinctive physical environment that wasn't translating digitally. We built a brand identity and website rooted in the school's own interiors, pulling colour and character directly from the building to create a digital presence that feels like Polam before a parent reads a single word.
challenge: "Polam School and Nursery had something many schools don't: a genuinely distinctive physical environment. Warm interiors, natural textures, specific greens and earthy tones that gave the school its own atmosphere. The problem was that none of it translated digitally. The website communicated nothing of what it actually felt like to be inside the school. For prospective parents making decisions about their child's education, that feeling matters."
process: "The brief was clear: build an identity and a website that parents recognise as Polam the moment they arrive. Not a generic school template, not institutional navy. Something rooted in the actual school."
solution: "We started by studying the interior. Photography and video from inside the building became the foundation for every design decision. Colours were pulled directly from the environment: the earthy yellows, the specific greens, the warm neutral tones. The graphic system, a circle motif representing the school's four core values, was built from the same palette. Every element had a reference point inside the building."
results: "The website scaled this across a large number of pages, all consistent in tone and visual language. The information architecture was built to guide prospective parents clearly through the school's offering, from nursery through to senior years, with every section reflecting the same warmth and character as the physical building. The result is a digital presence that feels like Polam before a parent reads a single word. A brand that doesn't just describe the school, it reflects it."
